Recipe Spotlight: Speedy Lemon & Peanut Rice

This classic South Indian favorite is incredibly fast to make, especially if you have leftover cooked rice. It’s light, flavorful, and travels beautifully.

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups cooked rice (preferably leftover and cooled)
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1/2 tsp urad dal (split black gram)
  • 1/4 tsp chana dal (split chickpeas)
  • Pinch of asafoetida (hing)
  • 8-10 curry leaves
  • 1-2 green chilies, slit (adjust to taste)
  • 1/4 cup roasted peanuts
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
  • Juice of 1 large lemon
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander, chopped (for garnish, optional)

Method:

1. Heat oil in a pan. Add mustard seeds. Once they splutter, add urad dal, chana dal, and asafoetida. Sauté until the dals turn light golden.

2. Add curry leaves and green chilies. Sauté for a few seconds until aromatic.

3. Stir in the roasted peanuts and turmeric powder. Mix well.

4. Add the cooked rice to the pan. Gently mix everything, ensuring the rice is evenly coated with the tempering. Cook for 2-3 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the rice is heated through.

5. Remove from heat. Add lemon juice and salt to taste. Mix gently.

6. Garnish with fresh coriander if desired. Pack warm into your tiffin!

FridgeToFork Tip: Pair this with a small side of plain yogurt or a simple cucumber salad for a complete meal.

More Quick Tiffin Ideas to Explore

  • Curd Rice (Thayir Sadam): Simply mix cooked rice with plain yogurt, a little milk, salt, and a quick tempering of mustard seeds, curry leaves, and ginger.
  • Vegetable Upma: A wholesome breakfast staple that works perfectly for lunch. Load it with your favorite veggies.
  • Besan Cheela (Savory Gram Flour Pancakes): Quick to make, nutritious, and can be served with chutney or pickle.
  • Paneer & Veggie Scramble: A quick sauté of crumbled paneer with onions, tomatoes, and spices, served with roti or bread.
