Recipe: Quick Aloo Bhaji (Spiced Potatoes)

This humble potato stir-fry is a testament to how incredible just a few ingredients can be. It's a perfect side dish or a light meal with roti.

Your 5 Key Ingredients:

  1. Potatoes
  2. Mustard Seeds
  3. Turmeric Powder
  4. Red Chili Powder
  5. Fresh Curry Leaves (optional, but highly recommended for authentic flavor)

Pantry Staples: Cooking oil, salt, water.

Instructions:

  • Peel and dice 2 medium potatoes into small, even cubes.
  • Heat 2 tablespoons of cooking oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat.
  • Once hot, add 1 teaspoon of mustard seeds and let them splutter.
  • Immediately add the diced potatoes. Stir well to coat.
  • Sprinkle in ½ teaspoon of turmeric powder, ½ teaspoon of red chili powder (adjust to taste), and salt to taste. Add a handful of fresh curry leaves, if using.
  • Mix thoroughly, then add about ¼ cup of water, cover the pan, and reduce heat to low.
  • Cook for 10-12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the potatoes are tender and cooked through. If needed, add a splash more water.
  • Uncover, increase heat slightly, and sauté for another 2-3 minutes until the potatoes are lightly browned and crispy.
  • Serve hot with roti, paratha, or as a side with dal-rice.
