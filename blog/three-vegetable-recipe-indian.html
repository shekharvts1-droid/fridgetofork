FridgeToFork's Special: The Three-Veggie Scramble (Bhurji Style)

This recipe is designed to be your go-to for any three leftover vegetables. It’s quick, adaptable, and bursting with Indian flavours.

Ingredients:

  • 1 tbsp cooking oil (mustard or vegetable oil preferred)
  • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1 small onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • Your 3 leftover vegetables, roughly 1.5 cups total (e.g., 1 small potato, 1/2 bell pepper, 1 carrot – all diced small)
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric powder (haldi)
  • 1/2 tsp red chili powder (adjust to taste)
  • 1 tsp coriander powder (dhania powder)
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped tomato or 1 tbsp tomato paste
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped (for garnish)

Instructions:

  1. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat. Add cumin seeds and let them splutter.
  2. Add the chopped onion and sauté until it turns translucent.
  3. Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook for another minute until fragrant.
  4. Add the harder of your three vegetables first (e.g., potato, carrot). Sauté for 3-4 minutes.
  5. Now, add turmeric, red chili powder, and coriander powder. Mix well and cook for 1 minute, ensuring the spices coat the vegetables.
  6. Introduce the softer vegetable (e.g., bell pepper) and the chopped tomato or tomato paste. Mix thoroughly.
  7. Cover the pan and cook on low heat for 8-10 minutes, or until all vegetables are tender. Stir occasionally to prevent sticking. Add a splash of water if needed.
  8. Once cooked, stir in the garam masala and salt. Mix well and cook for another 1-2 minutes.
  9. Garnish generously with fresh coriander leaves. Serve hot with roti, paratha, or as a side with dal-rice.

Pro Tips for Your Leftover Trio

  • Texture Grouping: Cook harder vegetables (potatoes, carrots, beans) first, then add softer ones (bell peppers, zucchini, leafy greens) to ensure even cooking.
  • Spice It Right: A basic mix of turmeric, red chili, and coriander powder is a fantastic starting point. Add a pinch of garam masala at the end for an aromatic finish.
  • Acid Boost: A dash of lemon juice or a sprinkle of amchur (dry mango powder) can brighten up the flavours, especially for richer vegetable combinations.
  • Consider a Base: Instead of just a dry sabzi, blend your cooked veggies into a simple curry, add them to a quick pulao, or even mash them into paratha stuffing!
