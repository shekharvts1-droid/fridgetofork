Leftover Dal: From Bowl to Brilliant!

Transform your everyday leftover dal into exciting, new Indian dishes and embrace a zero-waste kitchen!

Photo by Mario Raj on Unsplash

Don't Toss That Dal!

In most Indian households, a pot of comforting dal is a daily staple. But what happens when there's a little bit left at the bottom of the pot? Instead of letting it go to waste or forcing yourself to finish it, think of it as a hidden culinary gem! At FridgeToFork, we believe every ingredient deserves a second act, and leftover dal is a star waiting to shine.

Repurposing dal isn't just about reducing food waste; it's about unlocking incredible new flavours and textures that can surprise and delight your palate. From hearty snacks to light breakfast options, your leftover dal is far more versatile than you think!

Beyond the Bowl: Quick Transformations

The beauty of leftover dal lies in its ready-made flavour base. It already has seasoning, making it a fantastic foundation for various quick fixes:

  • Dal Paratha filling: Thick dal (especially moong or tur) can be mashed, mixed with some chopped onions, green chillies, and fresh coriander, then used as a delicious, protein-packed filling for parathas.
  • Dal Cutlets/Tikkis base: Combine mashed dal with boiled potatoes, breadcrumbs, spices, and finely chopped vegetables to create flavourful patties that can be pan-fried or shallow-fried.
  • Thickening Agent: Thin dal can be added to vegetable gravies or curries to thicken them and add a subtle, nutritious boost.
  • Soup Base: Whisk leftover dal with some vegetable broth, add finely chopped veggies, and simmer for a quick, nutritious soup.

Recipe Highlight: Spicy Dal & Vegetable Tikkis

Here’s a practical and delicious way to use that leftover dal, transforming it into irresistible Tikkis perfect for tea time or as a side with your main meal:

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup leftover cooked dal (any variety, preferably thick)
  • 1/2 cup boiled and mashed potatoes
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped onion
  • 1 tbsp finely chopped ginger-garlic
  • 1-2 green chillies, finely chopped (adjust to taste)
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, bell peppers)
  • 2-3 tbsp breadcrumbs (plus more for coating)
  • 1/2 tsp roasted cumin powder
  • 1/2 tsp chaat masala
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste (remember dal is already seasoned)
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped
  • Oil for shallow frying

Method:

  1. In a large bowl, combine the leftover dal, mashed potatoes, chopped onion, ginger-garlic, green chillies, mixed vegetables, breadcrumbs, roasted cumin powder, chaat masala, turmeric, and chopped coriander.
  2. Mix everything thoroughly until well combined. The mixture should be firm enough to shape. If too moist, add a bit more breadcrumbs. Adjust salt if needed.
  3. Divide the mixture into small portions and flatten them into tikkis (patties).
  4. Lightly coat each tikki with additional breadcrumbs for a crispier exterior.
  5. Heat oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat. Place the tikkis in the pan and shallow fry until golden brown and crisp on both sides.
  6. Serve hot with mint chutney, tamarind chutney, or ketchup.

Other Brilliant Dal Repurposing Ideas

  • Dal Dosa/Cheela: Blend thin leftover dal with a little rice flour, semolina, and water to form a batter. Add spices and make thin, savoury crepes (cheelas) for a healthy breakfast.
  • Dal Pakoras: Mix thick dal with gram flour (besan), chopped onions, spinach, and spices. Deep-fry spoonfuls to make crispy pakoras.
  • Dal Stuffed Sandwiches: Spread a thick layer of seasoned leftover dal between bread slices, add some cheese or veggies, and grill it for a quick, wholesome sandwich.

Pro Tips for Leftover Dal Success

  • Consistency is Key: If your dal is too thin for recipes like tikkis or paratha filling, simmer it down to reduce moisture or add a binding agent like mashed potato, breadcrumbs, or a spoonful of gram flour.
  • Spice it Up: Even if your dal was seasoned, don't hesitate to add fresh spices, herbs, or aromatics like ginger, garlic, green chillies, or chaat masala to boost the flavour profile for its new avatar.
  • Storage Savvy: Store leftover dal in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 2-3 days. Always ensure it smells and looks fresh before using.
