FridgeToFork Special: Spiced Egg & Onion Scramble (Masala Anda Bhurji)

Here’s our go-to recipe for a truly satisfying and quick meal:

Ingredients:

  • 3 large eggs
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 1-2 green chillies, finely chopped (adjust to taste)
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 cup finely chopped tomato (optional, but adds great flavor)
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder (Kashmiri for color, regular for heat)
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1/4 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil or ghee
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped (for garnish)
  • Salt to taste

Instructions:

  1. Heat oil or ghee in a pan over medium heat. Add the chopped onions and green chillies. Sauté until the onions turn translucent and slightly golden.
  2. Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook for another minute until fragrant.
  3. If using, add the chopped tomatoes. Cook until they soften and the oil starts to separate from the mixture (about 3-5 minutes).
  4. Reduce heat to low and add turmeric powder, red chilli powder, coriander powder, and garam masala. Sauté for 30 seconds, ensuring the spices don't burn.
  5. Whisk the eggs with a pinch of salt in a separate bowl. Pour the whisked eggs into the pan over the onion-spice mixture.
  6. Let the eggs set for about 30 seconds, then gently start scrambling with a spatula until cooked through but still moist. Avoid overcooking.
  7. Garnish generously with fresh coriander leaves. Serve hot with pav, roti, or toast.

Tips for the Perfect Bhurji

  • Don't Rush the Onions: Cooking onions until they are properly golden brings out their natural sweetness, adding depth to your dish.
  • Adjust Spice Levels: Feel free to increase or decrease green chillies and red chilli powder based on your heat preference.
  • Fresh is Best: Freshly grated ginger-garlic paste makes a noticeable difference in aroma and flavor.
  • Tomato Power: While optional, tomatoes add a lovely tang and moisture, making the bhurji richer.
  • Avoid Overcooking: Scrambled eggs should be just cooked through, not dry. Remove from heat as soon as they are firm.

Beyond the Scramble: More Egg & Onion Ideas

While the bhurji is a star, don't limit yourself! Try these:

  • Masala Omelette: Add finely chopped onions, green chillies, and a sprinkle of spices to your whisked eggs before frying.
  • Egg Curry Base: Sauté onions until golden, add ginger-garlic and spices, then tomatoes, before simmering with boiled eggs.
  • Onion Pakora with Egg Dip: Serve crispy onion pakoras with a side of creamy, spiced scrambled eggs for a unique twist.
